Cover your eyes. Touch. Guess.

Body Touch Game is a party game for 2–8 people on one phone. One person is the model, another one guesses. The model sees a random spot on the body and guides the guesser's hand there, while the guesser keeps their eyes covered. Then the guesser puts a pin on the 3D model where they felt the touch. Sounds easy? You'll be surprised how hard it is to tell an elbow from a forearm with your eyes closed!

HOW IT WORKS
• The model sees the target, the guesser covers their eyes
• The touch – exactly how and as long as the phone shows
• The guesser places a pin on a 3D model they can rotate and zoom
• The app measures the distance along the body surface – the closer, the more points. A perfect hit is 100 points and confetti!

WHAT'S INSIDE
• A realistic 3D body model – rotate it with your finger, zoom in, front, side and back views
• Four game modes: Easy, Medium, Hard and Hardcore – from large body areas to tiny spots
• Touch type: whole hand, two fingers, one finger or a tap; touch time from 5 to 30 seconds
• A random target or one chosen by the model
• Everyone plays with everyone – pairs change automatically
• Player profiles, rankings, statistics and a heat map of hits and misses
• Excluded zones – each player can switch off spots they don't want in the game
• Zone editor and custom points on the body
• Animated tutorial
• 12 languages, the game works offline

The game is free and shows full-screen ads between rounds (no more often than every few rounds). A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns them off for good.

Perfect for parties, evenings with friends and team-building events. Play with respect for everyone's boundaries.

Available in English, Polish, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Czech, Slovak, Ukrainian and Dutch.
